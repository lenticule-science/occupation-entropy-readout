# Occupation Entropy Readout

This is the union-facing service settlement of the Obsmetrological Services
Section. It projects a bounded computation grounded in separately owned
Obsmetrologia mathematics — the Boltzmann occupation-entropy readout of an
occupation list — across its signed A2A 1.0 boundary.

## Settlement law

- `lib/` carries this unit's law and roster; `content/` carries lasting office
  and service records; `ops/` carries the deployable systemd unit.
- An executable projection is not a formally extracted Lean program. Every
  response MUST distinguish source pinning from a proof that the JavaScript
  implementation is definitionally equal to Lean.
- The service MUST refuse operation if its admitted mathematical source bytes
  do not match the digest under which its conformance evidence was produced.
- The service owns its entropy-readout implementation and readout records. It
  never writes its mathematical supplier, its customer, or another
  settlement's ledger.
- The signed A2A 1.0 face (bin/serve-a2a.mjs, RMN mark parts) is the
  implemented carrier. ActivityStreams contract
  boundary, ActivityPub transport, x402 settlement, and public-network
  operation remain absent.
- Cost is a local measured resource readout, not a settled denomination or a
  price authorized for the whole firm.

## Roster

- **occupation-entropy-broker** — owns the A2A boundary, source
  attestation, conformance evidence, typed refusals, and truthful
  qualification of results.
